About Mitch Graves

Hi, I'm Mitch

I've worked in data for about 10 years, and nearly all of it has been in healthcare. I led reporting to federal agencies during COVID-19, measured clinical and financial outcomes at Virta Health, and worked on product strategy at athenahealth.

Along the way I've done a bit of everything: data architecture, product analytics, dashboards, and a lot of explaining numbers to executives.

How I Think About Data

Most data problems aren't really technical. The SQL is usually the easy part. The hard part is picking the right question, figuring out what the answer means for the business, and explaining it clearly enough that someone does something with it.

That's what I focus on with clients, and it's what I write about in The Everyday Analyst.
